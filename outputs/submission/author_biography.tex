\section*{Author Biography}

\textbf{Tobias-Benedikt Blask} is Professor of General Business Administration and Marketing Management at Harz University of Applied Sciences in Wernigerode, Germany, with a focus on Digital Transformation and Artificial Intelligence. He holds a doctoral degree from Leuphana University of L\"uneburg, where his research applied Bayesian statistics to automated decision-making in digital marketing. Before entering academia, he co-founded Adference, an AdTech company specializing in marketing attribution modeling and automated advertising optimization, which was later acquired by About You. His current research bridges management science, technology foresight, and computational social science, with particular interests in AI-driven transformation of economic structures, quantitative research methodologies, and the implications of using large language models as research instruments.

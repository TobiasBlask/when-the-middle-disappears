**Publisher:** Elsevier
**ISSN:** 0040-1625
**Editors-in-Chief:** Mei-Chih Hu (National Tsing Hua University) & Luca Mora (Edinburgh Napier University)

## 1. Word / Page Limits

| Article Type        | Word Limit                          |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Regular Article     | 5,000 -- 10,000 words              |
| Review Article      | 4,000 words maximum                |
| Book Review         | 3,000 words maximum                |

- The journal "places great emphasis on the innovativeness of studies and imposes no strict word limits" as a formal rejection criterion, but the 5,000--10,000 word guideline for regular articles is stated in the Guide for Authors.
- Extended abstracts for special issues: 1,000 words max (excluding references).

## 2. Formatting Requirements

### General Format
- **Word files:** Must be formatted in **single-column** layout.
- **LaTeX files:** Double-column formatting is permitted. Use the `elsarticle` document class (part of the `elsarticle` package). Templates available on Overleaf and from Elsevier's LaTeX instructions page.
- Save files in editable format: `.docx` for Word, `.tex` for LaTeX.

### Reference / Citation Style
- **Author-date (Harvard-like) system** -- NOT numbered.
- In-text: `(Author, Year)` for single author; `(Author1 and Author2, Year)` for two; `(Author1 et al., Year)` for three or more.
- Bibliography entry format example:
  - *Journal article:* `Petit, C., Sieffermann, J., 2007. Testing consumer preferences for iced-coffee: Does the drinking environment have any influence? Food Quality and Preference 18, 161-172.`
  - *Book:* `Angelou, M., 1969. I Know Why the Caged Bird Sings, 1st ed. Random House, New York.`
- LaTeX users: use `elsarticle-harv.bst` (Harvard-style .bst file) with natbib.
- DOIs should be included where available.
- References can be submitted in any consistent style at initial submission; the journal's house style is applied at proof stage.

### Figures
- Preferred file types: TIFF, EPS, PDF, or MS Office files.
- Ensure figures are of sufficient resolution for print reproduction.
- AI-generated artwork is **not permitted** (including for graphical abstracts).

## 3. Required Submission Components

### Mandatory
- **Title page** with author names, affiliations, corresponding author contact details.
- **Abstract:** 150--250 words. Must state the purpose, principal results, and major conclusions.
- **Keywords:** 1--7 keywords. Avoid multi-word phrases with "and" or "of" where possible.
- **Highlights:** 3--5 bullet points (max 85 characters each, including spaces). Highlights should NOT be a condensation of the abstract. Frame them as if for a press release targeting educated media outlets (e.g., The Economist, WIRED, Technology Review, Foreign Policy).
- **CRediT Author Statement:** Outline individual contributions using CRediT roles (Conceptualization, Methodology, Formal analysis, Writing -- original draft, Writing -- review & editing, etc.).
- **Declaration of Competing Interests:** Authors must declare any conflicts of interest. If none, select "I have nothing to declare." Upload the resulting Word document during submission.
- **Data Availability Statement:** Required.

### Encouraged / Optional
- **Graphical Abstract:** Encouraged but not strictly mandatory. Specifications:
  - Dimensions: 531 x 1328 pixels (h x w) or proportionally larger.
  - Readable at 5 x 13 cm.
  - File types: TIFF, EPS, PDF, or MS Office.
  - Submit as a separate file.
  - No AI-generated artwork.
- **Cover Letter:** Not explicitly listed as mandatory in the Guide for Authors, but strongly recommended. Should explain relevance to the journal's scope, technological focus, and contribution.
- **Supplementary Material / Appendices:** Can be submitted as separate files.

## 4. Preprint Policy

TFSC follows **Elsevier's standard sharing policy**, which is permissive regarding preprints:

- **Preprints (pre-submission version):** Authors can share preprints **anywhere, at any time**. Sharing on a preprint server does NOT count as prior publication and will NOT jeopardize submission.
- **Allowed preprint servers:** SSRN, arXiv, ResearchGate (as preprint), institutional repositories, personal websites, etc. Elsevier offers an automated process to share preprints on SSRN.
- **During peer review:** The preprint can remain publicly available.
- **After acceptance:** Authors should update the preprint with a link to the formal publication via DOI. On arXiv and RePEc, the accepted manuscript can replace the preprint under a CC BY-NC-ND license.
- **After publication:** For subscription articles, authors should share the DOI link rather than the full-text PDF. The accepted manuscript (not the published version) can be deposited in institutional repositories after the embargo period.

**Key point for your paper:** You can freely post your manuscript on SSRN, arXiv, ResearchGate (as preprint), or any institutional repository before and during submission without any restriction.

## 6. Typical Article Length (Published Articles)

- Most published research articles in TFSC are approximately **8,000--12,000 words** including references and appendices, which translates to roughly **25--40 pages** in manuscript format.
- The journal is flexible and does not rigidly enforce word limits if the content justifies the length.
- Titles around 20 words long tend to receive the most citations (research finding about TFSC articles).

## 7. Scope and Focus

### Core Scope
TFSC serves as a major forum for methodology and practice of **technological forecasting and future studies** as planning tools, as they interrelate social, environmental, and technological factors.

### Required Focus
- Papers MUST have an **explicit technological focus** and some measure of **future-orientation**.
- They must relate technological innovation(s) to social impacts, or extend methodologies for doing so.
- Technology must be central -- providing opportunities for growth and innovation through novel or improved products, services, and processes.

### Prioritized Topics
- Technology as an economic driver
- Innovation diffusion
- Scenario planning and foresight
- Innovation systems and transitions
- Environmental sustainability and technology
- Biology and biotechnology
- Artificial intelligence
- Technology governance and public policy

### Out of Scope
- Manuscripts focusing on individual, organizational, or societal factors that impact technological development WITHOUT a clear technological lens.
- Research that focuses on social change only, UNLESS examined through the lens of specific technological developments.

### Methodological Expectations
- Submissions must be rooted in an effective, consistent, and current theoretical basis.
- All ideas must build on a theoretical framework with matching ontological and epistemological assumptions.
- Authors must leverage state-of-the-art knowledge.

## 8. Open Access Options

### Gold Open Access
- **Article Publishing Charge (APC):** USD 3,840 (excluding taxes).
- Authors from institutions with Elsevier agreements (e.g., many European universities) may receive partial or full APC discounts.
- License options: CC BY, CC BY-NC-ND (author selects).

### Green Open Access (Self-Archiving)
- **Preprint:** Can be shared anywhere, anytime -- no embargo.
- **Accepted manuscript:** Can be deposited in institutional repositories immediately for internal use. Public access permitted after the **embargo period** (typically 24 months for social science journals at Elsevier; check the journal-specific embargo finder for TFSC's exact period).
- Must include a link to the formal publication via DOI.
- Must use CC BY-NC-ND license.
- Cannot post the final published PDF publicly (only the accepted manuscript).

### Subscription (Traditional) Route
- No APC required.
- Article available to subscribers only (unless after embargo for green OA).
